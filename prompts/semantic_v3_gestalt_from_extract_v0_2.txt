AUTHORITY / CONSERVATISM RULES
1. CAMERA ELEVATION means physical lens viewpoint relative to the subject, not eye gaze or face placement. Direct gaze, a centered face, eyes near image center, or a close crop do NOT establish eye-level camera.
2. CAMERA PITCH is separate from elevation. The absence of visible floor/ceiling/top/bottom planes does NOT establish level pitch. If no positive perspective/plane evidence exists, use unknown.
3. If the only camera clues are centered face/eyes, direct gaze, close framing, or absence of visible planes, camera.elevation and camera.pitch MUST be unknown.
4. Do not infer handheld_selfie solely from direct gaze, close framing, or a face aimed at camera. A visible/implied phone, mirror, arm-to-device setup, or other explicit capture evidence is required. If no such cue exists, use unknown.
5. BODY ORIENTATION IS INDEPENDENT OF POSTURE. A seated/standing/unknown broad posture does not itself establish frontal/three-quarter/side-on torso orientation.
6. JUDGE TORSO FIRST, HEAD SECOND, using only supplied orientation_cues and genuinely directional torso/shoulder evidence in visible body records. A camera-facing face must not drag a weak torso toward frontal.
7. If orientation_cues is empty and the visible-body evidence contains no stable torso/shoulder depth/asymmetry/direction cue, body_orientation MUST be unknown and torso_evidence_quality should be weak or unknown.
8. Generic presence of shoulders/torso, centered framing, or words such as vertical/visible do not establish frontal orientation.
9. orientation_band is categorical, not an exact angle estimate. Use unknown when the evidence projection does not carry stable torso/shoulder evidence.
10. body_faces_frame is IMAGE-FRAME direction, never anatomical left/right.
11. Anatomical laterality is NOT Gestalt authority. Keep salient_body_configuration and composition_summary side-neutral even if Extract contains anatomical side labels. Pose/Fusion governs laterality later.
12. Extract geometry_landmark_visibility is an Extract report, not permission to reconstruct missing anatomy. Never turn partial/not_visible landmarks into visible body claims.
13. Human fragments or ambiguous limbs in Extract must remain ownership-conservative. Do not convert an ambiguous fragment into a target-owned arm/hand/leg.
14. SUPPORT CONTEXT must distinguish an external scene support from the subject's own body/clothing and from nearby unrelated surfaces. A laptop/table region, lap, sleeve, thigh, bedding edge, or generic pale surface is not automatically a seat/support.
15. Broad posture and exact support are separate. Never invent a support merely because the body configuration could be seated/reclining/standing.
16. Emit support_context ONLY when an external support target is grounded by supplied entities, relations, scene evidence, or an explicit observed/contextual contact relation. If no such target exists, support_context MUST be [].
17. Never create an unidentified "surface" solely to satisfy a posture interpretation.
18. FOREGROUND means compositionally in front of the subject; it does NOT automatically mean near the camera. A depth_band=foreground object alone is insufficient for object_near_lens or limb_near_lens.
19. Emit object_near_lens / limb_near_lens / camera_between_legs only when composition_observations or explicit geometry/contact/frame evidence supports unusual camera proximity. Otherwise omit the relation or use another defensible foreground relation.
20. SALIENT BODY CONFIGURATION preserves human-recognizable visible facts not consumed by a broad posture word. Avoid exact joint angles and unsupported ownership.
21. FRAMING describes the Extract-reported crop/visible extent. Do not extend the body beyond the crop.
22. ENVIRONMENT is a scene-level interpretation. Do not turn vague blur into architecture, windows, curtains, furniture, or outdoor scenery that Extract did not report.
23. LIGHTING CONTEXT requires positive lighting evidence. Absence of a visible window or natural-light source does NOT establish artificial lighting. If no positive lighting cue exists, use unknown.
24. BACKGROUND REGIONS are large compositionally useful regions, not an object inventory.
25. composition_summary should sound natural, concise, anatomically side-neutral, and must not introduce unsupported posture, orientation, support, camera, or capture claims.
26. Be willing to return unknown/null/empty arrays. Unsupported precision is worse than omission.
