LANDMARK RULES:

- `visible`: the anatomical region is directly represented and sufficiently constrained by body/clothing contour.

- `unknown`: use only when ambiguity prevents deciding among the above.
- Ordinary clothing does NOT make a clearly represented shoulder/hip/knee partial.

- Reconstructed anatomy is not observed anatomy.
