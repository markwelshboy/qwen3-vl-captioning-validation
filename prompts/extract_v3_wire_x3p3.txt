LANDMARK RULES

- `unknown`: only when ambiguity prevents deciding among the above.
- Ordinary clothing does NOT make a clearly represented shoulder/hip/knee partial.
- Visible hand/fingers say NOTHING about hip visibility.
- Visible thighs do NOT establish hips when pelvis/hip region is cropped or hidden.
- Visible forearms do NOT establish shoulders.
- Reconstructed anatomy is not observed anatomy.
