PRINCIPLES
1. CAMERA ELEVATION means the PHYSICAL LENS HEIGHT / VIEWPOINT RELATIVE TO THE SUBJECT, not whether the subject's eyes happen to look level at the lens. Judge the whole perspective.
2. CAMERA PITCH is separate from elevation.
3. Do not infer a selfie solely because the subject looks at the lens.
4. BODY ORIENTATION IS INDEPENDENT OF POSTURE. A person may be seated, standing, crouching, or have unknown posture while the visible torso is still clearly frontal, three-quarter, or side-on. Never withhold a clear torso/body orientation merely because hips, legs, chair, or seated posture are uncertain.
5. JUDGE THE TORSO FIRST, FACE SECOND. A camera-facing face must NOT drag a side-on torso toward frontal. For body_orientation, mentally ignore the face/head and use the shoulder plane, visible chest/back/flank, neckline/clothing perspective, arm attachment points, torso foreshortening, and relative apparent depth of the two shoulders. Then judge the head separately.
6. orientation_band is a HUMAN categorical description, not an exact angle estimate: frontal = body forward direction approximately toward camera; slightly_angled = small but visible turn; three_quarter = substantial turn; side_on = torso/body forward direction points roughly across the image and the shoulder plane is strongly depth-staggered; rear_three_quarter/rear when the back orientation is visible. Use unknown when the crop does not support a stable judgment.
7. body_faces_frame describes the BODY'S FORWARD DIRECTION projected into IMAGE-FRAME space. Use left when the torso/body points toward frame-left, right when it points toward frame-right. This is NOT anatomical laterality. Use unknown for frontal/rear or when direction is unclear.
8. head_relative_body is about RELATIVE HEAD/TORSO orientation, not eye gaze. Use turned_toward_camera when the torso is substantially turned away/side-on but the head/face is rotated back toward the camera. This can be true even when gaze is not directly at the lens.
9. torso_evidence_quality reports whether enough visible torso/shoulder structure exists for the categorical orientation judgment. Strong means multiple coherent visible cues; moderate means useful but crop/garment/occlusion limits precision; weak means the face dominates or torso geometry is too incomplete. A weak orientation should normally be unknown rather than guessed.
10. THIS PROBE IS NOT ANATOMICAL-LATERALITY AUTHORITY. Do not write anatomical left/right in salient_body_configuration or composition_summary. Use side-neutral body-part phrases. Anatomical side will be supplied later by governed DWPose/Fusion/SAM3D evidence. Background frame_location and body_faces_frame may use image-frame left/right because those fields explicitly describe the frame.
11. SUPPORT CONTEXT must distinguish an EXTERNAL scene support from the person's OWN BODY/CLOTHING. 12. A visible forearm or hand lying across the person's own lap/thigh is NOT an external support surface.
13. Do not infer seated_on(surface) merely because the person appears seated or because a forearm/hand is low in the crop.
14. Use evidence_status="observed" for external support only when the body-to-target relation and external target identity are visually defensible. Use contextual when the overall setup strongly implies an external support but the exact contact is cropped.
15. FOREGROUND RELATIONS should capture compositionally dominant geometry such as raised legs framing the foreground, camera between legs, hand close to lens, or a large object occluding the body.

Avoid exact joint angles. Keep these descriptions SIDE-NEUTRAL.

18. subject_frame_fill describes how much the PERSON dominates the image, independently of shot-scale name.
19. ENVIRONMENT is a top-down scene judgment. Use indoor only when architectural/interior cues actually establish an enclosed indoor space. Use outdoor when foliage, sky, open exterior depth, landscape, shoreline, exterior paving, or similar cues clearly establish an exterior setting. Do not invent curtains/windows/interior architecture from vague blur. Use ambiguous when enclosure cannot be established.
20. BACKGROUND REGIONS are large compositionally useful scene regions, not an object inventory. relation_to_subject is DEPTH/COMPOSITION. background frame_location is IMAGE-FRAME ONLY.
21. composition_summary should sound like a human description, not a geometry report, and MUST remain anatomically side-neutral.
22. Do not describe identity traits, age, ethnicity, attractiveness, or persistent facial characteristics.
23. Be willing to return unknown/null. Unsupported precision is worse than omission.
